Install

Four steps, about a minute.

Bulong runs entirely on your Mac. It asks for two macOS permissions — microphone to hear you, accessibility to type the result where you're working.

  1. 1

    Open the download

    Double-click the Bulong.dmg, then drag Bulong into your Applications folder.

  2. 2

    Launch & allow the microphone

    Open Bulong from Applications. When macOS asks for the microphone, click Allow — that's how it hears you.

  3. 3

    Turn on Accessibility

    So Bulong can type into any app, grant Accessibility once: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, then switch Bulong on. Without it, results are copied to your clipboard instead.

  4. 4

    Pick your language & key

    Choose Filipino, English, or another language, set a hotkey, and the first speech model downloads once. Then press the key and talk.
